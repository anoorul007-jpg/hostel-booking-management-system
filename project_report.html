Executive Summary / Abstract

Efficient student housing allocation is a vital operational pillar for educational universities and collegiate institutions. Traditional manual room allotment suffers from communication delays, double-booking errors, lack of real-time vacancy tracking, and sluggish grievance resolution workflows.

The Hostel Booking and Management System (HostelMaster) is an enterprise-scale, automated web portal specifically designed to digitize a hostel campus of 4 distinct residential blocks (Blocks A, B, C, D), spanning 6 floors per block and 10 rooms per floor, yielding a total capacity of 240 rooms and 1,200 students (5 students per room).

The platform delivers a comprehensive set of automated modules: interactive color-coded floor explorers, public student onboarding with simulated SMS OTP verification, bed-level allocation algorithms, 14 cataloged amenities, an 8-category complaint tracking system with photo upload support, student payment histories with downloadable tax receipts, a structured vacating lifecycle, and a Chief Warden administration panel with live KPI analytics and CSV report generation.

Core Impact: Fully replaces paper registers with automated database records, guarantees zero double-bookings, strictly enforces room capacity ceilings, and enables real-time bed recalibration upon student departures.

The backend persistence is governed by an indexed SQLite schema (and synced with an offline localStorage datastore) ensuring instantaneous data read/write latency.

3. Functional Modules & Implementation Details

3.1 Module 1: Interactive Room Explorer (rooms.html)

Provides a visual floor plan navigator across all 24 floors. When an administrator or student picks Block A and Floor 2, the grid renders rooms 201 through 210 with live bed occupancy bars. Clicking "View Details" opens a modal showing individual Bed #1 through Bed #5 status, indicating whether each bed is vacant or occupied by a named resident.

3.2 Module 2: Registration & SMS OTP Verification (register.html)

Collects complete student information including college department, academic year, guardian contact, permanent address, and ID proof. An interactive 6-digit verification modal generates a time-sensitive OTP with a 60-second countdown, preventing fraudulent account creation.

3.3 Module 3: Bed Booking & Demo Payment Gateway (booking.html)

Allows registered students to reserve specific vacant beds, calculate total fees (rent + refundable security deposit), and complete checkout through a simulated payment gateway (UPI, Card, NetBanking). Generates a printable fee receipt formatted with transaction IDs and authorized signatures.

3.4 Module 4: 14 Core Hostel Facilities (facilities.html)

Detailed catalog covering high-speed optical Wi-Fi (500 Mbps), 240+ CCTV surveillance cameras, 24/7 security with biometric turnstiles, RO drinking water plants on every floor, smart automated laundry, modern dining hall serving 4 meals daily, silent study lounge, TV entertainment lounge, RFID parking bays, dual generator power backup, daily housekeeping, attached bathrooms, centralized hot water, and indoor/outdoor sports arenas.

3.5 Module 5: Maintenance Ticketing & Complaints (complaints.html)

Students can file maintenance issues under 8 categories (Water, Electricity, Wi-Fi, Bathroom Cleaning, Room Maintenance, Food Quality, Security, Other) with photo evidence upload. Admins can update tickets from Pending to In Progress and Resolved with technical notes.

3.6 Module 6: Student Dashboard (student-dashboard.html)

Residents can review their allocated room and bed position, inspect roommate profiles, track past payment invoices, download printable receipts, file complaints, and submit formal room vacating notices.

3.7 Module 7: Admin & Warden Portal (admin-dashboard.html)

Offers live KPI cards, visual management of all 240 rooms, student onboarding approvals, complaint resolution queues, announcement publishing, and one-click export of room inventories and student rosters to CSV.
